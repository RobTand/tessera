# T-16 dense launch at rates 9 to 14 (contract v51)

Refs #750, item 4 of the T-16 worker.

The value library's dense launch now reads column rates 9 to 14. This page
holds the receipts for that change: the SASS comparison that shows the rate-1
to rate-8 kernels did not move, the numerics tests, and the timing of the new
rates against BF16.

## Scope

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Kernel source | `src/tessera/serving/csrc/routed_fused_window.cu`, sha256 `910e621e43aff771…` |
| Baseline source | the same file at `817125d795` (contract v49), sha256 `28f2ecc989793983…` |
| New instantiations | value library, dense launch only: one-run rates 9 to 14 and the pairs 8/9 to 13/14 |
| Unchanged | both routed launches (gate/up and down) in every library; both E4M3 libraries' dense launch |

## Geometry

- The dense launch holds the 28-word slot of rates 13 and 14 at three word
  stages: 80,144 B of sm_121's 101,376 B opt-in block.
- The two-table gate/up launch cannot hold rate 9's 20-word slot even at two
  stages (101,840 B), so the routed launches stay at rates 1 to 8.
- Rates 15 and 16 are excluded by geometry. Their 15-bit and 16-bit windows need
  64 KB and 128 KB tables, which leave the one-table block one word stage and
  none.

## SASS: the rate-1 to rate-8 kernels did not move

`decode_rows` reads an NZ-word window per lane: three words up to rate 8, four
at rates 9 to 12, five at rates 13 and 14. A first version used one array of NZ
words at every rate. It compiled to the same instructions at rates 1 to 8, but
it moved ptxas's schedule in 21 of the E4M3 libraries' 244 kernels. The lead
and the T-8 worker are timing those kernels now, so this version keeps the
three named window words and their statements at rates 1 to 8 and uses the
array only at rates 9 to 14.

The comparison builds each library from both sources with the extension's own
flags and compares every kernel's SASS:

```
TORCH_INCLUDE=<torch include dir> experiments/t16_sass/sass_identity.sh \
    <v49 routed_fused_window.cu> src/tessera/serving/csrc/routed_fused_window.cu <out dir>
```

Host nvcc 13.0.88, `-gencode arch=compute_121,code=sm_121`:

| Library | Kernels identical to v49's | New kernels | Kernels with a spill |
|---|---|---|---|
| value | 76 of 77 | 24 (101 in all) | 0 of 101 |
| E4M3 | 107 of 107 | 0 | 0 of 107 |
| E4M3-MMA | 137 of 137 | 0 | 0 of 137 |

The one value kernel that differs is the dense launch's (7, 8) pair,
`routed_fused_kernel<false, 2, true, false, 7, true, 64>`. With labels and
virtual registers renamed, its PTX differs only in shared-memory address
arithmetic: LLVM reassociated a constant offset (v49 adds 36864 to a sum, v51
adds 32768 first and 4096 after), which leaves v51 with four fewer `add.s32`
instructions and every other opcode count equal. After ptxas, the SASS
instruction count (1,632) and the register count are equal. The GPU oracle and
the timing A/B below cover it.

The new dense kernels at rates 9 to 14 use 94 registers and do not spill.

Scope of this receipt: both sides were built with the same host nvcc. The
served build compiles in the serving image, whose nvcc is not the host's. The
comparison shows that the source change is neutral under one compiler; it does
not compare the image's binaries.

The branch was rebased onto master `87b4187c0c` (contract v50) after this
comparison. v50 changed no file under `csrc/` and not `serving/ext.py`, and
`routed_fused_window.cu` hashes to the same `910e621e43aff771…` after the
rebase, so the comparison holds as written.

## Numerics

GPU tests in the pinned image: PB `d8668bb9` before the rebase (629 tests,
0 failed) and a rerun on the rebased head (pending; the PR names its key), over
`tests/test_routed_fused_window.py`, `tests/test_dense_fused_window.py`,
`tests/test_window_gemm_grouped.py`, `tests/test_native_window_moe.py`,
`tests/test_serving_native_window.py` and `tests/test_window_gemm.py`. They
include:

- the exact one-hot decode oracle at every value rate above 8 (q256 2176 to
  3584) and at q256 1920, the (7, 8) pair;
- a derived error bound against the Triton twin at rates above 8, on
  `o_proj`-sized and smaller shapes;
- graph capture and replay above rate 8;
- random fractional mixes inside every pair up to 13/14, with the upper-rate
  columns placed at random (the dense MIX oracle);
- the routed launches' refusal above rate 8, by name.

1. The A/B of the (7, 8) pair against v49's kernel: base, new, base, new, at
